Joe Taylor’s soft, strange worlds

The Berlin animator and illustrator whose soft, strange worlds never quite sit still.

WordsArt Desk Read1 min Sources8

Joe Taylor draws the kind of creatures you half remember from a dream: soft-edged, slightly lopsided, cheerfully strange. A British animator and illustrator based in Berlin, he started out as a graphic designer before illustration and animation took over.[3][1]

Much of his work moves. His illustrations loop and morph, so his portfolio never quite sits still.[4] That style has carried him to clients including Apple, Netflix and TED-Ed,[1][3] and onto Vinyl Moon record jackets, including a gatefold for Space Case with a rotating artwork spinner.[8] He also makes music as J Yerolota,[1] which might explain why so much of his work feels like it has a groove underneath it.

With fellow animator Alex Gamsu Jenkins, he made the 2022 video for “For Willow” by the Berlin house producer Moomin.[4][5][7] It opens on a barren landscape that slowly comes back to life, greenery creeping over everything until the whole world is lush and strange.[5]

For Creators Lab, Joe made the artwork for our first print issue: calm creatures, floating gardens, a cup of something warm. It is a gentle world, and a good one to spend time in.
