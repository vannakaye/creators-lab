The three typefaces this site is set in

One for the interface, one for reading and one for the name: the type behind Creators Lab.

WordsDesign Desk Read2 min Sources6
[ Contents ]DM Mono, for almost everythingNewsreader, for readingArchivo, for the nameWhy threeSources and notes

Creators Lab is set in three typefaces, and each has one job.

DM Mono, for almost everything

Every label, headline and button on this site is DM Mono, a monospaced family in three weights and three styles designed by Colophon Foundry for DeepMind, with creative direction from the DeepMind team.[1][2] It is loosely based on DM Sans, with lower contrast, and was added to Google Fonts in April 2020.[2] A monospace makes everything look like a record of something: a lab note, a set time, a catalogue number. That is the mood we wanted.

Newsreader, for reading

Long articles switch to Newsreader, a serif commissioned by Google Fonts and designed by Hugues Gentile with art direction by Jean-Baptiste Levée at Production Type.[3][4] It was made for continuous reading on screens, and it comes in optical sizes, so the letters change shape slightly between caption size and display size.[3] It joined Google Fonts in July 2020.[3]

Archivo, for the name

The CREATORS LAB wordmark is Archivo at its widest and heaviest. Archivo is a grotesque sans by Héctor Gatti and the Omnibus-Type team, originally designed for highlights and headlines and built to work in print and on screen at the same time.[5][6] We use it nowhere else.

Why three

One voice for the interface, one for reading and one for the name keeps the site calm. The only colour we add is a single electric blue, #2723F1. Everything else is black, white and grey.
